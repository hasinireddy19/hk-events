About HK Events

Who We Are

A Team That Understands India's Heart

HK Events was born from a simple belief — that every Indian celebration, regardless of the region, community, or tradition, deserves to be planned with the same love and grandeur.

Our team consists of planners and coordinators from across India who understand the nuances of a Telugu Pelli just as deeply as a Punjabi Shaadi, a Malayali Vivaaham as warmly as a Marathi Lagna, and a Bengali Biye as gracefully as a Rajasthani celebration.

We don't just plan events — we become a part of your family for that special day.

What Sets Us Apart

Personalized Design
